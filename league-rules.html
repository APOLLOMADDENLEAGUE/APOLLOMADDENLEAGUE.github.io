LEAGUE RULES

Current rules for the Apollo Madden League.

72 HOUR ADVANCE 3 TRADES MAX PLAYOFFS STREAMED
01

ADVANCE

League advances every 72 hours.

02

4TH DOWN

You may go for it if:

  • It is 4th & 6 or less
  • You are at or past the 50-yard line
  • You are down 14+ in the first half
  • You are losing at any point in the second half
  • The game is tied in the 4th quarter
  • You need a first down to end the game
NO 4TH-DOWN RESTRICTIONS IN THE PLAYOFFS.
03

CHEW CLOCK

  • Final 2 minutes of the first half
  • Final 4 minutes of the 4th quarter
04

POSITION CHANGES

Position changes must be realistic. Players can only move to unusual positions if they have played that position in real life.

  • WR → RB/TE only if realistic
  • CB/S → LB only if they have played LB

No unrealistic position abuse.

05

TRADES

  • User-to-user trades only
  • No trading with the CPU
  • Maximum 3 trades per team, per season
  • Follow the Trading 101 rules
06

GAMEPLAY

  • No custom playbooks
  • No scum kicking
  • No playing CPU games
  • No trading with CPU teams
07

QUITTING

If you quit before stats save, you will be punished.

08

PLAYOFF STREAMING

All playoff games must be streamed.

  • If neither player wants to stream, each player must stream one half
  • Refusing to stream your required half can result in a Force Loss
  • If the entire game is played without a stream, the winner may receive multiple player suspensions
09

INACTIVITY

3 GAMES OF INACTIVITY = REMOVAL FROM THE LEAGUE.
10

RULE VIOLATIONS / FORCE WINS

If your opponent breaks a league rule, you may leave the game immediately and receive the Force Win.

If you choose to continue playing, you accept the game result.

11

DEV GOALS

If your opponent quits before you can complete your dev goal, you have a CPU game, or you were unable to play the game, DM proof to an admin.

You may be allowed to complete the dev goal in your next game.

12

NO HANDOUTS

Everything must be earned.

You may not:

  • Ask for or give free wins
  • Ask for or give free stats
  • Intentionally run commit, use FG block, or purposely allow stats
  • Threaten to quit unless someone gives you stats
  • Pre-arrange stats or outcomes

Running up the score is allowed. Giving things away intentionally is not.

Punishment may include player suspensions or removal from the league.

Players who report cheating with proof may receive league rewards.

EVERYTHING IS EARNED. NOTHING IS GIVEN. BE LEGENDARY.
13

COACHING ABILITIES

You may not stack multiple copies of the same coaching ability.

Example: New All-Madden Effort can only be equipped once.

Stacking abilities may result in player suspensions or removal from the league.
